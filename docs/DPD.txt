New Class: Artificer
Has a unique toolbox and arcane firearm

Toolbox:
	The toolbox is leveled up in a few different ways:
		You can spend Arcane Materials to level up the toolbox.
		The toolbox gains experience as you use spend charges using it.
	The toolbox can be used to craft bullets and bombs (just regular bombs).
	Arcane Materials can be found in the dungeon, and you can "deconstruct" weapons, armor, etc. into Arcane Materials.
	You can also disarm dungeon traps with your toolbox to get Arcane Materials.
	Crafting doesn't consume toolbox charges, and neither does deconstructing items.
	Using an affixed artifact, disarming/creating traps, and summoning sentries does.
	Toolbox can go up to level 15 or so, maybe.
	You can affix an artifact to your toolbox:
		Will bring the toolbox level up to the artifact level (not adding, just the max).
		Will give you the effect of the artifact as if the artifact were the level of your toolbox.
		If the affixed artifact would level up, the toolbox levels up instead.
		Using the affixed artifact costs toolbox charges (don't activate the toolbox charges food talent if eating from an affixed horn).
		Removing the artifact destroys it, yields lots of Arcane Materials.

Recipes:
	1 material -> 2 bullets
	1 material + [thing] -> 2 special bullets
	3 materials -> bomb
	5 materials -> repair kit? For sentries, etc.?
	3 materials -> 3x Weapon lacing: each one takes 1 turn to apply and makes next melee attack deal max damage
	3 materials -> 2x Armor bracing: each one takes 1 turn to apply and makes your armor roll max damage reduction on its next attack

After the first reset:
	You choose a secondary class and you get some of the T1 and T2 talents of that class as T5 and T6 talents, plus some T5 and T6 talents for your main class
		You will not get the Destiny nor Fate talents.
		Have a talent associated with trinkets
	Tengu's mask gives you an "Archetype" with more talents
	The Dwarf King's Crown gives you a Heroic Weapon that has special abilities

After the second reset:
	You get a skill tree to spend levels on instead of talents
	Tengu's Mask grants you another skill tree tab full of stuff related to your subclass and archetype
	The Dwarf King's Crown gives you another skill tree tab full of stuff related to your Heroic Armour and Weapon

After the third reset:
	You get a "Boons" tree, basically a set of very powerful buffs/abilities. You only get one every 10 levels, or something similar, and you get to choose one of three options or something like that.
	Tengu's Mask grants you another Boons tab full of thing related to your subclass and archetype
	The Dwarf King's Crown gives you another Boons tab full of stuff related to your Heroic Armour and Weapon

After the fourth reset:
	You get a "Paragon" screen, which is a small set of buffs that you can just level and improve infinitely. You get one every level, but only after the skill tree is fully exhausted.
	Tengu's Mask grants you a new set of Paragons for your subclass and archetype
	The Dwarf King's Crown grants you a new set of Paragons for your Heroic Armour and Weapon
